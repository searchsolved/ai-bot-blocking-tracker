How to read this

  • This is declared policy (robots.txt), not enforcement, and it is not a measure of whether a site actually gets cited.
  • Blocking a training crawler is a licensing decision and does not remove a site from AI answers. Blocking an AI search crawler does.
  • Google AI Overviews and AI Mode are not in this data. Their eligibility follows ordinary Googlebot indexing. Google-Extended governs Gemini training only.
  • Each industry list is built the same way: candidate domains from public registries and enumeration, validated against the Tranco top 1M ranked-domain list, classified to the industry, then ranked. Per-industry pages carry the full method.
  • Domain Rating by Ahrefs, last refreshed 2026-08-12.
